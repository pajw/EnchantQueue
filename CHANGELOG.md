## Enchant Queue 0.4.0

- The panel now docks to the right of the profession tabs instead of covering them, lined up with the top of the Enchanting window and matching its height.
- New note icon under the profession tabs shows or hides the panel. If you close the panel, it stays closed the next time you open Enchanting until you click the icon (or type `/eqq`) again.
- Restyled to match Blizzard's Professions window: Enchanting portrait, item slot with a quality-coloured border, tidier queue rows with icons and counts, and more rows on screen.
